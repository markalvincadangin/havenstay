# HavenStay Boarding House Management System (BHMS)
## Case Study

---

## 1. Introduction

Rapid urbanization has increased the demand for communal housing solutions such as boarding houses. While large real estate entities utilize advanced Property Management Systems (PMS), smaller operators often rely on manual paper records. This case study explores the development of **HavenStay BHMS** to standardize data management and optimize operational workflows.

---

## 4. Problem Identification

### 4.1 Observed Problems
- **Manual Inefficiency**: Managing tenant data and payments manually is error-prone and slow.
- **Inconsistent Records**: Discrepancies between room status and payment logs.
- **Limited Accountability**: Lack of user access control makes tracing actions impossible.

---

## 6. Opportunity for Improvement

By transitioning to a digital framework, HavenStay realizes the following:
- **Centralized Data**: Single source of truth for tenants and rooms.
- **Automated Billing**: Reduced manual computation and improved accuracy.
- **Audit Trails**: 42 database triggers on core tables (including BEFORE/AFTER snapshots) supporting full accountability for data changes.
